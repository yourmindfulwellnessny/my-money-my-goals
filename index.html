What kids will learn

Simple, hands-on lessons that connect money with patience, feelings and responsible choices.

  1. 🐷

    Why saving matters

    How small amounts add up — and why waiting can be worth it.

  2. ⚖️

    Needs vs. wants

    Telling the difference between what we need and what we'd like to have.

  3. 🫙

    Save, Spend, Share

    Splitting money into three jars, each with its own purpose.

    SaveSpendShare
  4. 🎯

    A goal and a 30-day plan

    Choosing one goal and making a simple 30-day savings plan.

We also talk about patience and the emotions we feel when we shop, so kids can make calmer, more confident decisions.
